Every planner I tried assumed I had unlimited evenings. Lumen starts from the opposite premise: you tell it the hours you can realistically give this week, and it fits the work into them.

## What I did

- Ran short interviews with eight classmates about how their week actually falls apart
- Designed a weekly canvas where blocks resize instead of overflow
- Built the planning view in Next.js with keyboard-first editing
- Wrote the empty states so a blank week reads as an invitation, not a failure

## What I learned

Constraint-first planning removes the guilt loop. When the plan can't exceed the hours available, missing a task becomes a scheduling problem instead of a character flaw.
