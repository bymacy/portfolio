Local jeepney and bus routes are learned by asking people, not by reading maps. Atlas tries to encode that word-of-mouth knowledge into something a newcomer can follow on a moving vehicle.

## What I did

- Mapped six commonly-chained routes with landmark cues instead of stop codes
- Designed a one-thumb card stack for the next three transfers
- Prototyped the 'you should get off soon' moment as haptics plus a single line of text
- Tested the flow one-handed, standing, on an actual commute

## What I learned

The hardest screen was the one shown while nothing is happening. Most of the design work went into what the app says during the fifteen quiet minutes between transfers.
