Watering on a seven-day timer kills plants slowly. Bloom asks two quick questions when you check in and shifts the next reminder accordingly.

## What I did

- Designed a check-in that takes under five seconds and two taps
- Illustrated eight plant states so the app can show, not describe
- Built a reminder model that widens or tightens the interval after each check-in
- Ran a four-week trial with six people and eleven plants

## What I learned

People answered honestly when the app never implied they'd done something wrong. The tone of the copy mattered more than the accuracy of the model.
