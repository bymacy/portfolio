## The ask

Announce the BuildHers+ Philippines mentorship initiative across Facebook,
LinkedIn and Instagram, from one layout, without the headline breaking on any
of them.

## What I made

- A primary 1:1 layout with a bold display headline and a soft gradient field.
- Story and banner crops that keep the mentorship wordmark intact.
- A caption template so the organisers could ship follow-up posts themselves.

## What I learned

Designing for reposts means designing for compression. I raised the contrast
between the headline and the gradient after the first test upload came back
muddy.
