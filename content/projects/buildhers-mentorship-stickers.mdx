## The idea

A programme feels real when there's something physical attached to it. The
sticker sheet gave mentors a badge and mentees a reason to start a
conversation at meetups.

## What I made

- Twelve stickers on one sheet: role badges, reaction stickers, a mentor card.
- One shared outline weight so the set reads as a family at laptop scale.
- Print-ready cut lines plus transparent PNGs for Slack and Discord.

## What I learned

Sticker packs live or die on the small ones. The 1-inch badges were used far
more than the big hero sticker, so I rebalanced the sheet toward them.
