The club needed one mark that worked on a race bib, a shirt back and a phone screen. Everything else followed from that constraint.

## What I did

- Drew a wordmark that stays legible at 12mm and at 2m
- Set a two-colour system so shirts stay cheap to print
- Built a lightweight Astro landing page with the weekly route list
- Made a simple poster template the club can fill in each week

## What I learned

Designing for the smallest and largest sizes first made every decision in between easy. The screen version was the last thing I drew, not the first.
