## Why I made it

Note apps are built for people who want to keep everything. I wanted the
opposite: somewhere to put one small thing at the end of the day, and let the
rest go. The metaphor came first — a jar, a dark garden, a light you catch and
then release.

## What I designed

- A single capture screen. No folders, no tags, no title field.
- A garden view where each entry is one firefly, dimming a little each week.
- A weekly release ritual that clears the jar and keeps the habit light.

## What I learned

The hardest part was resisting features. Every review I ran ended with someone
asking for search, and every time I added it the app stopped feeling like a jar
and started feeling like a database. I kept it out.
