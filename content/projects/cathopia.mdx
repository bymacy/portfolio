## The pitch

*It's meow or never.* A stray cat walks out of a dead desert town looking for
catHopia — a place other cats swear they've seen. The joke is that the player
never finds out whether it exists.

## What I made

- Title screen and key art in a 32-colour dusk palette.
- HUD, pause menu and dialogue box, all built on an 8px pixel grid.
- A short style guide so the sprite work stayed consistent across the team.

## What I learned

Pixel UI punishes vagueness. At this size a control either reads instantly or
not at all, so every icon went through five or six passes before it survived
being shown to someone cold.
