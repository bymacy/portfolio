## The problem

Chore apps are built like project management software, and roommates are not a
project team. The tone was the whole design problem: how do you nudge someone
about the dishes without sounding like a manager?

## What I designed

- A wordmark and colour system warm enough to live on someone's fridge.
- A rotation view that shows whose turn it is, not who is behind.
- Reminder copy written in plain sentences, never a red badge.

## What I learned

Softening the tone made the app *more* effective, not less. In testing, people
responded faster to "kitchen's yours this week" than to an overdue counter.
