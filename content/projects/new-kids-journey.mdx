## The brief

Build the interface for a cozy RPG about being the new student. The first ten
minutes decide whether a player stays, and in this genre those ten minutes are
almost entirely menus.

## What I designed

- Inventory, map, dialogue and relationship screens on one shared grid.
- A controller-first navigation model, then adapted for mouse.
- An onboarding sequence that teaches the map before it teaches combat.

## What I learned

I ran the menus past six players who had never seen the game. Four of them
tried to close the map with the same button they used to open it, so I made
that work instead of teaching them otherwise.
