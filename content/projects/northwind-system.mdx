Three teams were shipping pages for the same organisation and none of them matched. Northwind is the smallest set of decisions that could make them agree.

## What I did

- Audited every live page and collected 41 distinct colour values
- Reduced the palette to nine tokens with documented intent, not just hex
- Built twelve components in React with typed props and Storybook stories
- Wrote a one-page adoption guide so nobody had to read the whole repo

## What I learned

A design system is mostly a naming exercise. Once the tokens had honest names, the wrong usages became obvious in review without anyone having to argue.
