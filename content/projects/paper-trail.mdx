Journalling apps tend to grow into dashboards. Paper Trail resists that: one entry, one page, and no streak counter anywhere.

## What I did

- Designed a writing view with no chrome until you move the cursor
- Built autosave with an explicit 'saved' state so the silence isn't unnerving
- Added a search that surfaces entries by mood tag rather than by date
- Deliberately shipped no analytics, no streaks and no reminders

## What I learned

Removing the streak counter changed how I tested it. With nothing to score, the only measure left was whether I still opened it in week three.
