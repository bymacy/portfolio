The restaurant had a menu photo posted to a social feed every Tuesday. The site needed to be as easy to update as that photo, and considerably easier to read.

## What I did

- Set the menu in MDX so the owner edits plain text, not layout
- Photographed six dishes against one consistent backdrop
- Built a reservation link that falls back to a phone number when the form is unavailable
- Kept the whole page under 90KB of JavaScript

## What I learned

Handing over a site means designing the editing experience too. The MDX files ended up with more comments than the component code.
